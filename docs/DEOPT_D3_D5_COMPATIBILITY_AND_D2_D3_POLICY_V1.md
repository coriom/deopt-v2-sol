# DeOpt D3 ABI resolution and D2/D3 operator decision

**Decision status:** technical recommendation; no economic value, DAG amendment, Safe transaction, Timelock action, or trading release is approved by this report.

At pinned Base Sepolia block **49,923,585** (`0x42f0460e2c6532f4b33915fae9afda2b882ce62c3e7c79fda669b837d35fa954`, 2026-10-25 05:36:25 UTC), D3 still points to `0xc0f019005a25524a34F2Ee8839DCDCC50715DD7B`. This is the historical *collateral* RiskModule, with Timelock owner and OPS Safe guardian. Its base token is mUSDC and its `collateralConfigs(mUSDC)` getter returns `(10000,true)`. D3's deployment constructor explicitly committed that pointer. D4 independently points to replacement perp RiskModule D5. Frozen Solidity under `src/` is unchanged from `25c36670883604c1ef5229642ee6548aea6796c3`.

## ABI decision

D3's `IRiskModuleConfigView` requires `baseCollateralToken() returns (address)` (`0x20d0d069`) and `collateralConfigs(address) returns (uint64,bool)` (`0x8cf18e64`) [CollateralSeizer.sol](../src/liquidation/CollateralSeizer.sol). The latter is the public mapping getter in [RiskModuleStorage.sol](../src/risk/RiskModuleStorage.sol). D5 [PerpRiskModule.sol](../src/perp/PerpRiskModule.sol) calculates collateral using Vault factors; it has no `collateralConfigs(address)`. A pinned public `eth_call` to D5 for that selector reverted. On an isolated fork, D3's nonzero seizure planner worked with its current risk pointer and reverted after locally changing the pointer to D5.

This is a **governance wiring assumption error**, not evidence that D3 was deployed with the wrong RiskModule. The approved DAG currently calls for `D3_RISK: D3.setRiskModule(D5)` and makes C13 depend on it. The source does not require that pointer for C13: C13 only sets D4's seizer pointer. The preferred remedy is to keep D3 on the compatible, already deployed collateral RiskModule and seek explicit approval for the [proposed DAG correction](../artifacts/perps_v2_replacement_deployment/d3_risk_dag_amendment_proposal_v1.json). The proposal replaces the D3-to-D5 action with a read-only compatibility gate after C20 and D3_TOKEN, while retaining C13's separate Safe/Timelock review. The approved DAG is untouched. If the operator rejects any ongoing dependency on the older collateral RiskModule, an adapter or replacement would require a new deployment and renewed getter, collateral-weight, owner, Vault/Oracle, D3-pointer, C13, and liquidation reviews; that is a larger alternative, not necessary on current evidence.

The mismatch matters operationally. [PerpEngineSeizureLib.sol](../src/perp/PerpEngineSeizureLib.sol) catches a reverted D3 plan and returns zero. [PerpEngineLiquidationLib.sol](../src/perp/PerpEngineLiquidationLib.sol) may then seize the settlement asset directly, so an outer liquidation need not revert. Multiasset seizure may be lost and shortfall/insurance/bad-debt outcomes may differ. D4's current seizer pointer is zero, liquidation is paused, migration is OPEN, and D4 lacks Vault authorization. C13 and liquidation release remain blocked. Future collateral changes must be coordinated across the legacy module, Vault, D3 and oracle before any release.

The current D3 mUSDC config reads `(spread=0, enabled=false, isSet=false)`, **but unset acts as enabled with zero spread** because the collateral RiskModule weight is 10000 bps. The proposed 5 bps spread is at the high end of D3's source-comment example for stable tokens, not a protocol mandate. With a 100.000000 mUSDC effective *penalty* target, a 9995 bps discount requires 100.050026 mUSDC gross seized; the extra 0.050026 mUSDC leaves the trader and reaches the liquidator. The D4 liquidation penalty is separate. Base-token mUSDC requires no cross-token conversion; any additional token needs Vault support, an enabled collateral weight, and fresh oracle valuation under D3's 600-second setting. D3's setter alone does not validate Vault support.

The D2 proposal changes all five PERP tiers so a future tier root cannot expose negative maker fees without another review. Current tier-0 defaults are 0.5/3 bps. For a 100.000000 mUSDC notional matched trade, the proposed maker pays 0 and taker pays 0.020000 mUSDC. D2 has no opening/closing fee dimension: D4 charges both matched sides using maker/taker status and notional. OPTION fees, RFQ discounts, and fee-consumer activation are outside this approval. D2 `consumeFees` is accounting-only; D4 would later transfer positive fees between Vault accounts to the configured recipient. A zero ProtocolFeeVault disables its accounting hooks and does not route revenue to InsuranceFund. Any withdrawal or rerouting of Timelock-owned internal credits requires a separate reviewed action.

The legacy FeesManager's mUSDC rebate budget is **999,977 raw units**. Replacement D2 reads **zero**. The ledgers stay separate; no copied root, balance, liability, rebate funding or Insurance credit is proposed. A local negative-maker test reverted first for an unset funding account and then for insufficient new-D2 budget. This does not establish that future fees are backed until C24/C14/C15 and Vault roles are separately reviewed.
